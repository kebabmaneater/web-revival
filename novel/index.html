The fluorescent hum was the only thing that filled the room. It was a stale, old and a cheap lamp that flickered.

It was also the only lifeline Nevan had in his room, sitting on his wheelchair. If that lamp were truly to fail he would lose his life. It was the only thing guarding him from Abyss, and it was failing.

He opened his browser tab and started to scroll the forums.

Nevan stared at the "Thread closed" message. His data plan had ran out on that post. That troll had uploaded high quality 4k footage and he foolishly downloaded it in this era of unprecedended network speeds! He would have to wait a few hours to get his data back. He had racked quite the hugner though.

Nevan pushed his chairs, the wheels clicking over the grooves on the wooden board. He rolled into a small kitchenette. It was 3 AM, but time was just a concept that no longer mattered.. It was always very bright outside. In this district though, it was a bit dimmer. That was because the lights were going through a major operational upgrade. What did they upgrade? Who knows. That was muddled in paperwork.

...Speaking of paperwork, he opened his mail that he had collected earlier in the day. It was an acceptance letter from his dream University.

It was the one path that he had in life. His heart was weak, and he was quite melancholic. University had given his purpose and the anomalies had given him reason. They were the only thing he frequented in forums - who wouldn't frequent those beings? They literally ignore all knows physics and shatter our world view! It really wasn't fair against humans.

Well, not like it mattered to Nevan. He wasn't the one in front lines. Only 21%+ percent had the luxury to become more than a tier-0 civilian. That, along with his heart that got weak a year ago after a stroke, he didn't have a chance.

The sky was mellow the further you looked. It had been for hundreds of years. In a month or so, he would have to pack and to move to his dream University.
